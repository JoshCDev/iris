# Safe AWD basics

Safe AWD (Alternate Wetting and Drying) is a rice irrigation practice: wait
until the water table falls 15 cm below the soil surface, then irrigate back
to a flood of +5 cm. A perforated AWD pipe is used to watch the water table.
Safe AWD typically saves 15–30 percent water and lowers methane (CH4)
emissions without reducing yield. During flowering, the field must stay
flooded. IRRI describes this as the standard safe-AWD protocol used by IRIS
when the vegetative and grain-fill stages allow a dry-down to −15 cm.
This plot needs irrigation when the AWD pipe hits the −15 cm trigger.
Hold irrigation when enough rain is forecast in 72 hours. Methane drops
because AWD adds aerobic (not flooded) days. Why irrigate now? Why is
