# Plot questions (English)

When does this plot need irrigation? Irrigate when the AWD pipe hits the
−15 cm trigger; hold irrigation if enough rain is forecast in 72 hours.

Why irrigate now? Why is irrigation on hold? The scheduler reads water
level, growth stage, and the 72-hour rain forecast.

Why does methane drop? Safe AWD adds aerobic days, so methane (CH4)
production falls versus continuous flooding.

What does Blast mean for this plot's water? Blast likes a prolonged wet
canopy. Brown spot can rise with a deep dry-down. Bacterial leaf blight
(BLB) spreads with flowing water between plots.

How do leaf risk and water fuse on this plot? IRIS scores the leaf class
against this plot's AWD water state and wet weather (rule fusion, not a
separate product).
