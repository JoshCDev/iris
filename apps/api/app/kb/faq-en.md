# Plot questions (English)

When does this plot need irrigation? Irrigate when the AWD pipe hits the
−15 cm trigger; hold irrigation if enough rain is forecast in 72 hours
and the table is still above the hard floor.

Why irrigate now? Why is irrigation on hold? The scheduler reads water
level, growth stage, and the 72-hour BMKG rain forecast. A logistic
regression is only a second opinion for human review; it does not change
the BMKG total that the scheduler uses.

What if it rains for a long time and the water stays above the AWD band?
Do not drain. If the table is at or above 0 cm, the recommendation is
WAIT. Safe AWD waits for the water to fall by itself. Pumping out is not
the protocol. DRAIN is for harvest only.

Why does methane drop? Safe AWD adds aerobic days, so methane (CH4)
production falls versus continuous flooding. The season receipt numbers
are a simulation labelled [simulated]; literature meta-analyses report
larger CH4 cuts under deeper drying and are a separate label.

What does Blast mean for this plot's water? Blast likes a prolonged wet
canopy. Brown spot can rise with a deep dry-down. Bacterial leaf blight
(BLB) spreads with flowing water between plots.

How do leaf risk and water fuse on this plot? IRIS scores the leaf class
against this plot's AWD water state and wet weather (rule fusion, not a
separate product).
